Euler Motion Study

Every body moves
in its own orbit.

A visual reflection on motion, balance, and the quiet harmony of celestial paths.

وَهُوَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ اللَّيْلَ وَالنَّهَارَ وَالشَّمْسَ وَالْقَمَرَ ۖ كُلٌّ فِي فَلَكٍ يَسْبَحُونَ

“And He is the One Who created the night and the day, the sun and the moon—each travelling in an orbit.”

Qur’an 21:33

لَا الشَّمْسُ يَنْبَغِي لَهَا أَنْ تُدْرِكَ الْقَمَرَ وَلَا اللَّيْلُ سَابِقُ النَّهَارِ ۚ وَكُلٌّ فِي فَلَكٍ يَسْبَحُونَ

“It is not for the sun to catch up with the moon, nor does the night outrun the day. Each is travelling in an orbit of their own.”

Qur’an 36:40

وَالشَّمْسُ تَجْرِي لِمُسْتَقَرٍّ لَهَا

“And the sun runs on its fixed course for a term appointed.”

Qur’an 36:38

خَلَقَ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضَ بِالْحَقِّ ۖ يُكَوِّرُ اللَّيْلَ عَلَى النَّهَارِ وَيُكَوِّرُ النَّهَارَ عَلَى اللَّيْلِ ۖ وَسَخَّرَ الشَّمْسَ وَالْقَمَرَ ۖ كُلٌّ يَجْرِي لِأَجَلٍ مُسَمًّى

“He created the heavens and the earth in truth. He wraps the night around the day and the day around the night, and He has subjected the sun and the moon, each running its course for an appointed term.”

Qur’an 39:5

الشَّمْسُ وَالْقَمَرُ بِحُسْبَانٍ

“The sun and the moon move according to precise calculation.”

Qur’an 55:5
